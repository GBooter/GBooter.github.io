Privacy Policy – Personal Material Manager

Effective date: 2026-09-06

1. Local Data

All material, server, lending and history data you enter is stored locally under %LOCALAPPDATA%\PersonalMaterialManager\data (SQLite databases, images, backups). Nothing is uploaded to the cloud.

2. Optional Peer Sharing

When enabled, the app listens on LAN (TCP 9001) and syncs materials only with instances you explicitly authorize; no internet transmission is involved.

3. Network

Loopback REST API for local tooling; optional outbound refresh to configured peer addresses; optional update checks.

4. Logs

Local log and crash diagnostic files are kept on your machine only.

5. Uninstall

Uninstall keeps your data; delete the data folder manually to remove it.

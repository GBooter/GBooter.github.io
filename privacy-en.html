Privacy Policy – Personal Material Manager Effective date: 2026-09-06 1. Local data: All material, server, lending and history data you enter is stored locally under %LOCALAPPDATA%\PersonalMaterialManager\data (SQLite databases, images, backups). Nothing is uploaded to the cloud. 2. Optional peer sharing: when enabled, the app listens on LAN (TCP 9001) and syncs materials only with instances you explicitly authorize; no internet transmission is involved. 3. Network: loopback REST API for local tooling; optional outbound refresh to configured peer addresses; optional update checks. 4. Logs: local log and crash diagnostic files are kept on your machine only. 5. Uninstall keeps your data; delete the data folder manually to remove it. 6. Contact: dxbxyz@hotmail.com
